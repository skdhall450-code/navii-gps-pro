# Rajasthan Phase 1

Rajasthan Phase 1 publishes district-level GPS tracking guides for all 41 current districts listed by the Government of Rajasthan.

The inventory follows the post-reorganisation structure, including Balotra, Beawar, Deeg, Didwana-Kuchaman, Khairthal-Tijara, Kotputli-Behror, Phalodi and Salumbar. Districts reversed during the reorganisation are not published as competing routes.

Each district page includes:

- English and Hindi-intent GPS tracking keywords.
- Four local discovery locations and a unique fleet workflow.
- Self-canonical metadata and one H1.
- WebPage, Service, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data.
- Links from the Rajasthan state hub and automatic sitemap inclusion.

Automated source and rendered-page checks lock the 41-district inventory, prevent duplicate routes, and verify indexability, metadata, schema and internal links.
