# Kerala Phase 1 SEO coverage

Kerala Phase 1 publishes district-level GPS tracking guides for all 14 Kerala revenue districts.

Each guide includes:

- unique route and fleet context for the district;
- four reviewed local discovery locations;
- English and Malayalam-intent search keywords;
- canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata;
- WebPage, Service, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data;
- links from the Kerala state hub and back to the hub;
- an official district administration reference;
- automatic sitemap inclusion and source/rendered-page validation.

The regional labels are editorial navigation groups. The pages do not claim a NAVII GPS office or guaranteed installation availability in a district.
