# Kerala Phase 2 SEO coverage

Kerala Phase 2 publishes 28 curated city and town GPS tracking guides: two reviewed locations in each of Kerala's 14 districts.

Each location guide includes:

- a unique operational workflow and route checklist;
- English and Malayalam-intent keywords;
- links to its Kerala state hub, parent district and paired location;
- canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata;
- WebPage, Place-scoped Service, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data;
- automatic sitemap inclusion;
- source-only and rendered-page checks that lock the reviewed inventory.

The pages are planning guides. They do not claim a local NAVII GPS office or guaranteed installation availability.
