# Madhya Pradesh Phase 2

Madhya Pradesh Phase 2 publishes 110 priority city and town SEO guides: two reviewed locations in each of the state's 55 current districts.

Each route uses the district-scoped pattern `/gps-tracker/madhya-pradesh/{district}/{city}` and includes:

- English and Hindi-intent GPS tracking keywords.
- A unique local fleet workflow, canonical metadata and one H1.
- WebPage, Service, FAQ and breadcrumb structured data.
- Links to the Madhya Pradesh hub, parent district and paired city or town.
- Automatic sitemap inclusion.

The source and rendered-page checker prevents duplicate routes, derives the reviewed city selection from the locked Phase 1 district inventory, requires exactly two records per district, verifies internal links and confirms canonical and structured-data output.
